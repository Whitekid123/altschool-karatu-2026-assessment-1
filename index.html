Biography

I'm Oshin Praise Oluwapelumi, a mechanical engineering graduate of Oduduwa University. For my thesis I built an AI-assisted aerodynamic drag prediction model using PointNet and FastAPI, which was my first real step from traditional engineering into software and machine learning.

Alongside my studies I've been building and working with technology teams. I'm a Software Engineering Intern at Flurzi, where I'm building the PlayVault Game Hub landing site, and I serve as Technical Lead and Frontend Developer overseeing a six-person development team. I also work on automated content pipelines at EndNovel, and I've built AI-integrated web apps such as a background remover, an AI virtual stylist and AI chat tools, as well as client-style sites for restaurant and e-commerce use cases.

Outside of code, I'm the Cowrywise Campus Lead for Oduduwa University, managing student ambassadors and financial literacy initiatives, and I volunteer teaching chess to local students.

Why AltSchool Africa

I chose AltSchool to turn the building I already do into a solid, properly structured foundation.

  • To go deeper than AI-assisted coding. I build many of my projects with AI assistance, and I want to fully understand the programming, databases, APIs and system design underneath them.
  • For a structured, hands-on program. AltSchool's School of Engineering offers a clear path, practical projects and a community of learners, which will help me grow from a builder into a well-rounded engineer.

My Goals

  1. Build strong software engineering skills. Develop a solid foundation in programming, databases, APIs, and system design.
  2. Combine engineering and AI. Build AI-integrated products and tools that solve real problems, drawing on my mechanical engineering and machine learning background.
  3. Build real-world projects and grow professionally. Apply what I learn to practical work, contribute to engineering teams, and become a well-rounded full-stack engineer.
